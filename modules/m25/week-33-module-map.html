MODULE OVERVIEW

Who blinked in Cuba, and why did the world survive?

Ninety miles from Florida, in October 1962, two men held the fate of hundreds of millions. This week follows the shooting wars in Asia that grew out of the Cold War, and then the island of Cuba and thirteen days that nearly ended everything.

QUICK CHECK-IN
A submarine officer refused to agree to fire a nuclear torpedo. What does restraint cost a person in a moment like that?
WHAT’S AHEAD
1
Lesson 1: Mao’s Victory, Korea, and Vietnam. How the Cold War left Europe and became a shooting war in Asia.
2
Primary Source 1: Kennedy, Address on the Cuban Missile Crisis (October 22, 1962). The president tells the nation about the missiles and announces the quarantine.
3
Lesson 2: Castro and the Cuban Missile Crisis. How a small island brought the two superpowers to the edge of nuclear war.
4
Primary Source 2: Khrushchev to Kennedy (October 26, 1962). The Soviet leader writes privately to the president and offers a way out.
