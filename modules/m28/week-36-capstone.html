28.7 · QUARTER 4 CAPSTONE
A Smaller World
One essay on the years from the Second World War to the present.
🎯 WHAT YOU ARE DOING

The Quarter 4 Capstone is one essay that brings the whole quarter together, from the Second World War to the opening years of this century. You will choose one of three questions, make an argument, and defend it with evidence from the lessons and the primary sources you have read.

❓ CHOOSE ONE QUESTION
  1. Between 1945 and 1991 the two superpowers never fought each other directly. Was that mainly the work of wise leaders, of the fear of nuclear weapons, or of luck?
  2. What may a people do when its own government wrongs it, and what may other nations do about it? Defend your answer with at least two of the cases of the quarter, such as South Africa, the American South, Yugoslavia, or Eastern Europe in 1989.
  3. Did the great turning points of this quarter turn more on the choices of particular men and women, or on conditions they did not control?
📝 WHAT YOUR ESSAY MUST DO

Write 800 to 1,200 words in Word. Give your essay a title. State your answer in the first paragraph, and keep it clear to the end.

  1. Use specific evidence from at least four different weeks of the quarter, naming people, decisions, and dates.
  2. Quote from at least two of the primary sources of the quarter. Put each quotation in quotation marks and cite it in a footnote.
  3. Include one counterfactual. Name a moment, say what might have gone differently, and say what you think would have followed.
  4. Answer the strongest objection to your view. Say what a thoughtful person on the other side would point to, and why you are still not persuaded.
  5. Say plainly what the evidence cannot show and what you are guessing.

Some of this quarter’s history involves great suffering. Write about it plainly and with respect. You will be graded on the quality of your argument, not on which question you choose or which side you take.

📤 HOW TO SUBMIT

Write your essay in Word and save it to your OneDrive folder. Then open this assignment in Canvas, choose the OneDrive tab, select your file, and submit.

M365 reminder: name your file like this: LastName_FirstName_Q4Capstone_WHSP. Save it in Optima → History → WorldHistory_Spring. Name your sources in footnotes (M365 Skill You’re Practicing: Inserting a Footnote in Word).

If you chose to work with a group in the module introduction, submit one shared essay and list every group member’s name under the title. Otherwise, write alone. Both paths use the same rubric.

✅ HOW YOUR CAPSTONE WILL BE SCORED
Criteria Excellent (4) Proficient (3) Developing (2) Beginning (1)
Understanding of the material
5 pts		 States the source’s claims and the lesson’s facts accurately and precisely, with short quotations used correctly. Accurate on the main points. Small gaps or loose wording. Partly accurate, or leans on summary instead of the source’s own words. Major errors, or little connection to the source and lessons.
Quality of reasoning
5 pts		 Builds each claim from evidence, weighs more than one explanation, and gives a counterfactual that is plausible and tied to the facts. Claims are supported. The counterfactual is reasonable but thinly argued. Claims are asserted more than shown. The counterfactual is a guess with little support. Little reasoning, or the counterfactual is missing.
Clarity and precision of expression
5 pts		 Clear, organized, and exact. Each question is answered in its own labeled part. Mostly clear. Occasional vagueness or wandering. Hard to follow in places, or parts are missing. Unclear or disorganized.
Intellectual honesty
5 pts		 Names what the source cannot show and what the writer cannot know. Does not claim more than the evidence supports. Notes some limits. Occasionally claims more than the evidence supports. Rarely notes any limit. Treats guesses as facts. Overclaims throughout, or presents invented details as fact.
Total:
20 pts total
