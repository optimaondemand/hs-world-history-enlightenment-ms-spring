MODULE OVERVIEW

Was Dunkirk a victory or a defeat?

In the spring of 1940 the war became something no one had foreseen. In six weeks Germany broke the army that had held it for four years in the last war, and a British army escaped across the sea with little more than its rifles. This week follows Poland, France, Dunkirk, and Britain standing alone.

QUICK CHECK-IN
In May 1940 some British leaders wanted to ask Hitler for terms, and Churchill said no. What would you want from a leader in such an hour?
WHAT’S AHEAD
1
Lesson 1: Poland, France, and Dunkirk. How Germany broke the French army in six weeks, and how the British army escaped by sea.
2
Primary Source 1: Directive No. 21, Operation Barbarossa (December 18, 1940). Hitler orders his armed forces to prepare to destroy the Soviet Union.
3
Lesson 2: Britain Alone, and Hitler Turns East. A nation refuses to give in, and a dictator makes the biggest gamble of his life.
4
Primary Source 2: Churchill, “We Shall Fight on the Beaches” (June 4, 1940). The prime minister tells Parliament what Dunkirk means.
WHAT YOU WILL TURN IN
  • Module Introduction survey (ungraded).
  • Reading Quiz: seven questions on both lessons and both primary sources.
  • Discussion: Was Dunkirk a victory or a defeat?
  • Module Review survey (ungraded).
